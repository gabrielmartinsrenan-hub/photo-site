02 / The Artist

Observing the unscripted stillness between moments.

Based between New York’s Hudson Valley and Paris, Elena Vance approaches photography as a tactile study of human presence and natural light. With a background in art history and analog darkroom printing, her imagery favors deliberate pacing over fleeting trends.

Whether documenting an intimate wedding on the Amalfi Coast, photographing an architectural monograph, or crafting editorial portraits for independent fashion houses, every frame is composed with restraint, tonal depth, and emotional honesty.

Primary Mediums

Hasselblad X2D 100C, Leica M11 Monochrom, and Contax 645 Medium Format Film (Kodak Portra 400 & Ilford HP5).

Selected Recognition

Featured in Kinfolk, Cereal Magazine, Architectural Digest, and PDN 30 Emerging Photographers.
